Hướng dẫn họcHow to learn

Eight cooking lessons, Vào Bếp Nhà Em. Each one has four parts:

  1. Listen

    Hear how to make the dish in English, then the steps read aloud in Vietnamese.

  2. Learn the words

    Key ingredients and verbs, each with a pronunciation guide. Tap a word to hear it.

  3. Follow the steps

    Short Vietnamese steps with a plain English line.

  4. Play

    Put the steps in order, listen and choose, say it aloud. Up to 9 stars per dish.

Tổng quan · Overview and goals

What's inside

  • Eight home dishes we cook with families on the trip: two with pork, two with chicken, two with beef and two vegetarian, from Hanoi, Hội An and central Vietnam.
  • Every lesson is explained in English, with Vietnamese steps read in the school's recorded voice.
  • A pronunciation guide for the six tones, and three games per dish.

By the end, you can

  • Name the ingredients you'll see at the market and in the kitchen.
  • Follow, and say, simple cooking steps in Vietnamese.
  • Hear the six tones and read our pronunciation guide.
  • Join in when your host family cooks, and start on your short video with confidence.
Hướng dẫn · Study guide

Each session

  1. Short and regular: 20–30 minutes, two or three times a week. One dish a week gets you through all eight in two months.
  2. In order: listen, learn the words, follow the steps, then play.
  3. Sound on: tap any Vietnamese word to hear it, then say it out loud. Speaking matters more than tapping the right answer.

Good to know

  • The grey line under each Vietnamese word spells it the English way, Hanoi style. The small arrow shows the tone.
  • The “say it aloud” game uses your microphone if your browser allows it. If not, you listen and check yourself.
  • Your stars (up to 9 per dish) are kept on this device.
  • We'll share the video theme, the deadline and how voting works at the coffee talk. The phrases you learn here are a good start.

2016
Ten years with Educap in Vietnam

Since 2016 we have worked with Educap on programs that bring foreigners to Vietnam to explore the country and learn its language and culture before they take up work assignments there. Educap organizes these programs; our team develops their Vietnamese language learning materials. Về Nhà Em grows out of that work.

Về Nhà Em opens the platform to adult friends of Vietnam. Your feedback on the speaking lessons helps us build better speaking practice for the children.

Questions

Before you ask

When exactly is the trip?

We're still finalising the dates with our partners and will announce them as soon as they're confirmed. The program runs about ten days in Vietnam: around five days in Hanoi, then Da Nang and Hội An. Hanoi is 11 or 12 hours ahead of Toronto, depending on the season.

Do I need to speak Vietnamese already?

No. The online lessons start from zero and focus on what you'll use on the trip: greetings, food, prices, directions and the kitchen.

How do I get a sponsored place?

There are five. They go to the strongest learners on our online speaking lessons whose short video in Vietnamese is voted among the best. We'll share the video theme, the deadline and how voting works when the challenge opens.

Do I need a visa?

Yes. Canadian passport holders apply online for a Vietnam e-visa (about 3 working days). We help everyone apply. If you hold another passport, tell us in the form.

Can I stay on and work, for example teaching English?

You can stay longer at your own cost, within your visa. Working is a different matter: teaching in Vietnam needs a work permit and work visa sponsored by an employer, and you can't work on a tourist e-visa. The Da Nang session shows you the legal route, step by step.

I have allergies or a special diet. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Two of our eight dishes are vegetarian and the others can be adapted. Tell us in the form and we'll brief each host family before you arrive.

Who organizes Về Nhà Em?

CVCEC, the Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council, a federally incorporated non-profit in Toronto, organizes the whole program, in Canada and in Vietnam. Về Nhà Em is part of our Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em platform and builds on ten years of developing Vietnamese learning materials for Educap's programs in Vietnam, since 2016.
