How you'll learn

Four steps, online first, then for real

Every topic follows the same learning cycle. You practise each step on our platform before you fly, then live it in Vietnam with the people you meet.

    Read a short story aloud to catch the rhythm, tones and feeling of everyday Vietnamese.

2016
Ten years with Educap in Vietnam

Since 2016 we have worked with Educap on programs that bring foreigners to Vietnam to explore the country and learn its language and culture before they take up work assignments there. Educap organizes these programs; our team develops their Vietnamese language learning materials. Về Nhà Em grows out of that work.

Về Nhà Em opens the platform to adult friends of Vietnam. Your feedback on the speaking lessons helps us build better speaking practice for the children.

Questions

Before you ask

When exactly is the trip?

Summer 2027. We'll confirm the exact dates early in 2027. The program runs about ten days in Vietnam: around five days in Hanoi, then Da Nang and Hội An. You book your own flights. Land at Nội Bài airport in Hanoi and we'll pick you up. In summer Hanoi is 11 hours ahead of Toronto.

Do I need to speak Vietnamese already?

No. The online lessons start from zero and focus on what you'll use on the trip: greetings, food, prices, directions and the kitchen.

How do I get a sponsored place?

There are five. They go to the strongest learners on our online speaking lessons whose short video in Vietnamese is voted among the best. We'll share the video theme, the deadline and how voting works when the challenge opens.

Do I need a visa?

Yes. Canadian passport holders apply online for a Vietnam e-visa (about 3 working days). For sponsored participants we pay the fee, and we help everyone apply. If you hold another passport, tell us in the form.

Can I stay on and work, for example teaching English?

You can stay longer at your own cost, within your visa. Working is a different matter: teaching in Vietnam needs a work permit and work visa sponsored by an employer, and you can't work on a tourist e-visa. The Da Nang session shows you the legal route, step by step.

I have allergies or a special diet. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Two of our eight dishes are vegetarian and the others can be adapted. Tell us in the form and we'll brief each host family before you arrive.

Who organizes Về Nhà Em?

CVCEC, the Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council, a federally incorporated non-profit in Toronto, organizes the whole program, in Canada and in Vietnam. Về Nhà Em is part of our Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em platform and builds on ten years of developing Vietnamese learning materials for Educap's programs in Vietnam, since 2016.
